# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Travis and technically fluent readers use this site as a creative workbench, research notebook, and public record of essays, field notes, tools, and experimental interfaces. Paper Trails users are trying to inspect the provenance behind published thinking. Spacetime users are trying to compare topics across geography and time without losing the evidence trail underneath the visualization.

## Product Purpose

The product exists to make research, writing, and epistemic tools visible as working systems rather than portfolio decoration. Success means each surface shows real data, makes its source of truth clear, and helps a reader understand how design decisions, infrastructure, policy, and knowledge systems shape human outcomes.

## Brand Personality

Curious, rigorous, handmade. The tone should feel like an engineering notebook, studio journal, and public research desk: warm, precise, and candid about what the system knows or does not know.

## Anti-references

Do not make this feel like a generic portfolio, resume, SaaS landing page, demo dashboard, or decorative AI interface. Avoid fake progress, mock data, inert controls, browser-visible secrets, generic suggested prompts, and populated-looking empty states that imply nonexistent backend data.

## Design Principles

* Real data first: every visible control and visualization should connect to real state, real navigation, or a real endpoint.
* Provenance over polish theater: empty states should explain the missing backend condition instead of hiding it.
* Studio-journal craft: preserve the hand-drawn, material, documentarian feel while keeping product controls predictable.
* Compare without confusion: spatial, temporal, and graph comparisons should make their active mode and data source obvious.
* Warm precision: keep the interface humane and tactile, but let dense technical information stay scannable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Every shipped surface must meet WCAG 2.2 AA: at least 4.5:1 contrast for normal text and 3:1 for large text, graphical objects, and interactive component states. All animated surfaces must honor `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing nonessential motion. Timeline and globe controls must be reachable in a logical Tab order, expose a visible focus indicator, support their primary actions from the keyboard, and communicate state without relying on color alone. Focus must remain within a region only while that region is explicitly modal; ordinary reading and visualization surfaces must not create scroll or focus traps.
